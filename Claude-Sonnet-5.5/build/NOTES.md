# Hogwarts build notes (Claude Sonnet 5.5)

Workspace rules (SPEC.md): everything happens through the `blender-official` MCP against the live Blender 5.1.1.
Only this folder is touched. Build scripts live in `build/` and are exec'd inside Blender through
`execute_blender_code`; scratch renders go to `_work/` (deleted at the end). No imported assets, no image textures,
no add-ons: every mesh, shader, sky and volume is generated by the scripts in `build/`.

## Machine
RTX 5090 (OptiX), Ryzen 9950X, 96 GB RAM. Blender 5.1.1, Python 3.13, numpy 2.4.

## Concept
Mood: **moonlit night**. Cool teal/blue ambient, warm window and lantern glow, low mist on the lake and in the valleys,
layered highland ridges with aerial perspective. World units: metres, Z up, +X east (right in the hero view), +Y north
(away from the hero camera). Lake level z = 0. Castle plateau Z0 = 54 m, castle front faces south (-Y).
The moon is a real sun lamp (3.1 deg disc) low behind the castle (az 12, el 19.5); a cool raking fill sun from the
east-south-east (az 110, el 27) models the south faces and the crag; ~80 warm point lights come from the lamp registry.

## Layout (plan, x east, y north)
- Great Hall (long, west): x -205..-115, y -21..5, four corner turrets, buttresses, dormers, copper ridge cresting.
- Grand Tower (round, tallest): (-96, 8) R=20 with attendant turrets; library wing and gallery to the east.
- Right cluster x -30..+95: clock tower with readable clock face, tall spire tower, round/octagon towers, gatehouse.
- Viaduct: east from the gatehouse, 9 arches, parapet with lantern posts over every pier, over the east gorge.
- Boathouse at the waterline below the crag, quay wall, jetty and rowboats, switchback stair up the crag.
- Greenhouses on the lower terrace (z ~ 44), terrace walls stepping down the crag, rim walls and courtyards.
- West outcrop: Owlery with perched/flying owls and a covered wooden bridge on trestles; gamekeeper's hut with a smoking
  chimney and standing stones on the east moor.
- Not built (optional in the spec): Quidditch pitch, distant railway viaduct.

## Cameras (hw_cams.RIG)

hw_trees + hw_nature (conifer/rock/reed prototypes, scatter via geometry nodes), hw_lights (lamp registry -> lights),
hw_scene (collections/camera/render helpers), hw_cams, hw_comp (compositor: bloom, vignette, grade),

s08_props, s99_all (master).

## How to rebuild / render
- `import s99_all; s99_all.run('final')` rebuilds everything (about 40 s) and saves hogwarts.blend.
- `hw_render.chain_start()` renders the seven deliverables one after another (3840x2160, 100 %, 1024 samples, OIDN).
- `hw_debug.run_sweep(cams)` renders a back-face override and maps red pixels back to objects with ray casts.

## Geometry conventions
Faces are CCW seen from outside. Thin surfaces are closed slabs (`hw_geo.slab`, `hw_arch.gable_roof`): underside trimmed
at the ridge so nothing pokes through the top, eave fascia and verge boards included. Prototypes are authored on a
south-facing wall (outward -y) and instanced with `outward=(ox, oy)`; glass panes get per-pane random glow.
Verified with the back-face sweep: the remaining red pixels are single-sided details seen through open trusses/arches.

## Render settings of the deliverables
Cycles (OptiX, GPU), 3840x2160 at 100 %, exactly 1024 samples per pixel (adaptive sampling off), OpenImageDenoise with
albedo + normal passes, AgX with the "Medium High Contrast" look, per-shot exposure and depth of field from
`hw_render.SHOTS`, compositor glare + vignette. Volumes are ray-marched (`volume_biased`), which converges to the same image
as delta tracking here but renders the mist volumes about twenty times faster. One 4K image takes about 7 minutes.

## Gotcha: Collection Info sorts its children by name
The scatter objects (Forest, Rocks, Nature_ShoreVeg) pick prototypes with `Instance on Points` + `Pick Instance` and an integer
`variant` attribute. The Collection Info node returns the children sorted alphabetically, not in creation order. Prototype names
therefore have to sort like their index (`t{lod}{species}_...` for trees, `block*` < `boulder*` < `spire*` for rocks);
`hw_nature.make_proto_collection` asserts this. Before the fix the near forest got smooth low-detail cones, and one of them
stood in the hero foreground as a dark spike.

## Look decisions worth remembering
- Foreground conifers are spruce / fir only. Pines built from smooth blobs read as low-poly broccoli at every distance.
- Rock shading uses straight-edged Voronoi fracture blocks stretched along the bedding. Sine-wave "veins" looked like ivy.
- Hillside terracing comes from `hw_terrain.strata`; on grassy lake bluffs it is deliberately faint (`a_str`), fine bedding
  (`strata_fine`) applies to the crag flanks only.
- The boathouse quay wall stands in the water: `hw_terrain.register_quay` carves the ground 1.6 m behind the wall face so no
  rock lip pokes in front of the masonry, and rocks are kept out of that strip.
